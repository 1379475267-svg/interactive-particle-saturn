About / Project

Built as a cinematic browser experiment

This scene treats Saturn as a responsive field rather than a fixed object. Expansion increases luminosity, loosens orbital order, and pushes the ring system into a turbulent near-camera state.

Saturn is presented as a responsive particle field: larger scale, stronger light, less orbital stability, and a deliberate collapse into turbulence near the camera.

System / Structure

Kepler motion, light falloff, burst transition

The ring particles use elliptical orbital parameters and radius-dependent angular velocity. A scale threshold then blends in random perturbation and outward burst offsets to break the original pattern.

The system starts from ordered orbital motion, then blends toward noise and outward burst offsets once the expansion threshold is crossed.
